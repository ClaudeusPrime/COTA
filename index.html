Getting into the Super Stage

  • Stage is on the Festival Lawn between Turns 11 and 12.
  • COTA-bought race tickets include Friday and Saturday concert access.
  • GA entry: Infield Entrance near Tunnel 2 / The Ranch, or between Turns 10 and 11.
  • Upgrade holders need a wristband (Maroon 5 from Fri morning, Post Malone from Sat morning): Grand Plaza or Main Grandstand box offices, or Guest Services tents at Turn 9 / infield.
  • Alesso: Amphitheater, enter from the Grand Plaza and follow signs.

Super Stage floor / loge: bag rules

Tighter than race-day rules. Only these bags are allowed in upgraded concert areas:

  • Clear resealable plastic bag, up to 1 gallon
  • Clear tote, up to 12 × 12 × 6 in.
  • Small clutch or purse, up to 4.5 × 6.5 × 2 in. (needn’t be clear)
  • Lockers at the entrance to the upgraded areas.
Villages, views and extras

Around the Track

Fan villages

  • F1 Fan Zone (Grand Plaza): sim racing, pit-stop challenge, sponsor activations, a mini-ride (tickets sold on arrival).
  • The Saloon (Turn 2): Western bar with dueling pianos.
  • Texas Tailgate (Turn 3): sports-bar screens, bleacher seating, COTA Pong.
  • La Cantina (Turn 6): live cumbia, folklórico dancing, lucha libre, friendship bracelets.
  • The Ranch (infield, by Tunnel 2): rodeo area with live music, art cars, calf roping, hat customizing.
  • Lonestar Land (Tunnel Road, near Turn 2): BBQ, memorabilia, tattoos, and the World’s Largest Cowboy Hat photo op.

Tower Observation Deck

251 ft, behind the Amphitheater. $40 entry at the Tower base, plus an optional $20 champagne toast.

  • Thu & Fri: 12:30–5:00 PM
  • Sat: 9:00 AM–1:00 PM and 3:30–5:30 PM
  • Sun: 11:00 AM–6:00 PM

Where to watch (general admission)

  • Most popular GA spots: Turn 1 Hillside, Turn 11, and Turns 16–18 behind the COTA Tower.
  • Collapsible chairs are fine in GA viewing areas.
  • No sitting on grandstand steps (blocks foot traffic, fire hazard).
  • No flags or banners in the grandstands.
  • Giant video screens are placed around the circuit.
  • COTA says fans reach the best GA spots by arriving early.

Fan Track Walk and podium

After Sunday’s race, fans can walk the starting grid between Turns 20 and 2 for the podium celebration, across from the Main Grandstand. Enter the track only through designated access points: Turn 2, Main Grandstand East, or Main Grandstand West. Follow track officials. Opening ceremonies (driver introductions, anthem) run on track before the race.

Merch, power and paddock

  • Merchandise booths: Grand Plaza, Main Grandstand and Lonestar Land. COTA Karting store at the Main Grandstand.
  • Phone charging stations exist; ask a Guest Services team member to point you to one.
  • F1 Paddock and pits are restricted to credentialed guests. The Support Paddock is open to the public.

COTALAND (next door)

Austin’s new theme park opened Sept 26, 2026. Admission is separate from your race ticket.

  • Circuit Breaker: Texas’ only tilt coaster (48 in. min)
  • Palindrome: first U.S. infinity shuttle coaster (48 in.)
  • Soaring Eagle Zip Line over the main straight (extra fee)
  • Hot Lap, COTA Karting, mini golf (extra fee)

Grand PrixView Thursday

  • Limited areas open: Grand Plaza, COTALAND, Main Grandstand (full map to come).
  • Enter through the Grand Plaza gates.
  • Parking: Lots F and H are included with a Thursday ticket. A weekend parking pass does not cover Thursday.
  • Premium Garage parking can be bought through Ticketmaster.

Fan duty: cheer for Max

Cheering for Max Verstappen is not optional. When the grandstands start the “do do do do do do, Max Verstappen” chant, the Seven Nation Army-style fan favorite, you join in. Orange-clad crowds are tradition at his races, so expect to hear it all weekend, especially from Turn 1 Hillside and the Fan Zone. Only rule: no flags or banners in the grandstands.

Security checks every guest

Gate Rules

You can bring

  • Bags up to 12 × 12 × 20 in. Items can stay in the bag during screening.
  • ONE empty water bottle, 32 oz or smaller, no glass. Empty CamelBaks are fine.
  • Binoculars and blankets
  • Portable collapsible chairs; collapsible wagons (GA only)
  • Small hand-held umbrellas
  • Sunscreen in a plastic bottle (no aerosol)
  • Personal cameras with standard detachable lenses
  • Strollers
  • Event-related signs up to 11 × 17 in., no pole
  • Necessary medical items (inspected at entry)
  • Portable phone battery / power bank. COTA’s policies never mention them, allowed or banned. Several fan guides say to bring one and carry it in hand through the metal detector. COTA hasn’t confirmed it, so check first. Fully charge it, and keep a short cable in your bag.

Leave it at home

  • Outside food and drinks, including alcohol and 0% beer/cider
  • Coolers, thermoses, ice chests
  • Glass containers of any kind
  • Bags over 12 × 12 × 20 in., briefcases, luggage, computer bags
  • Tripods, monopods, bipods, detachable telephoto lenses; pro video gear
  • Non-collapsible chairs, tents, canopies
  • Drones
  • Weapons: guns, knives, pepper spray, handcuffs, nightsticks
  • Fireworks, flares, explosives
  • Illegal substances incl. marijuana; e-liquid / vape juice
  • Bikes, scooters, skateboards, skates, hoverboards, Segways, golf carts
  • Balls, frisbees, balloons, horns, radios, bells, whistles, instruments, laser pointers
  • Pets (service animals only)
  • Signs over 11 × 17 in. or on poles; unauthorized merch or promo material
  • Flags and banners in the grandstands
  • Revealing or offensive clothing (staff decide)

My packing checklist

Ticks are saved on this device only. Ear protection, shoes and rain layer are general tips, not COTA rules.

The water bottle question

COTA’s written policy bans glass, not metal. But thermoses are banned, and an insulated steel bottle can look like one at the gate. One fan guide says metal bottles get confiscated. Plastic is the safe call. Refills are free at Turn 1, Turn 3, Main Grandstand, Grand Plaza.

Smoking, re-entry, lockers

Smoking/vaping only in authorized areas 15 ft from entrances; never in covered areas or grandstands. Re-entry is allowed: scan out first, expect screening again. Lockers at the Grand Plaza entry gates: first come, first served, small fee.

Nothing gets returned

Items left at the entrance are not returned. Leave banned items at home or in your vehicle. Walk-through detectors speed entry, and bags are still searched. Gate staff have the final say.

Parking rules and routes

  • No pass, no entry to COTA Blvd. Pass goes in the vehicle before you arrive.
  • Closest lots are still a 15–20 minute walk to the gates (fan reports).
  • Lots C–H, K: TX-130 S (toll) to Exit 451, left on Elroy Rd, 2 mi to COTA Blvd, right.
  • Lots L, R, S: same exit and road, follow signs about 2 mi on Elroy Rd.
  • Lots A, N, T: TX-130 S to Exit 453, left on FM-812, about 1.5 mi.
  • From Houston/Bastrop: Hwy 71 W to the Kellam Rd exit, then Kellam Rd about 3 mi to Elroy Rd.
  • Tailgating allowed in lots, limited to one parking space. Camping only in designated lots with passes.

Official shuttles

  • Downtown (Waterloo Park): from $49 round trip.
  • Northeast (Travis County Expo Center): from $34 round trip.
  • Dedicated bus lane and a front-gate drop-off near the Grand Plaza entry gates.
  • Buses run continuously from 60 minutes before gates open.
  • Last inbound: 6:30 PM Fri and Sat. Return service ends 60 min after the music stops. COTA lists Sunday’s last inbound as 2:00 PM (the page itself looks inconsistent; confirm).
  • Longest Sunday transit: 9:30 AM–12:15 PM.
  • Max 8 passes per order, cards only, kids 3 and under free. Show your mobile ticket for a color-coded wristband.
  • Standby passes at pickup aren’t guaranteed.

Other ways in

  • Free trams run continuously around the perimeter and stop at several locations.
  • Bike: COTA publishes a bike route map.
  • BLADE helicopter from Creedmoor: about 5 minutes versus up to 3 hours by road, with SUV transfer to the Turn 1 VIP entrance. Reserve through BLADE.
  • No train or metro serves the circuit.

Uber, Lyft and taxis

  • North McAngus lot only. COTA Blvd is closed to rideshare.
  • Free tram from McAngus to the Grand Plaza front gates (fans report a 10–30 minute walk if you skip it).
  • Phone signal gets unreliable when the crowd leaves.

Getting out after the flag

LEAVE IMMEDIATELY

Heavy traffic on every road out. Leaving before the flag still lands you in the same jam.

WAIT 60–90 MIN

Reports say traffic clears noticeably. Alesso (5:30 PM) and the Fan Track Walk fill the time.

BOOKED SHUTTLE

Bus lanes. One 2025 reviewer was back at the hotel in 35 minutes.

RIDESHARE

2–3 hour waits at the McAngus Lot reported, with $150–$300 surge fares.

Bar length = relative wait. Worst reported lots: Lot F (single exit lane), Lot T (3+ hours), Lot N (exits onto busy Elroy Rd). COTA’s own advice: trips home take longer than usual. Exit times are fan and third-party reports; COTA publishes no figure.

Crowd size

2025 was a sellout, estimated at 400,000+ for the weekend (no official figure released). 2024 was estimated near 430,000. Promoter Bobby Epstein said in July that 2026 was on pace for a record year. Expect long lines at security, concessions and shuttles, especially Sunday.

From the airport and downtown

  • Austin-Bergstrom to the circuit is about 12 miles. One guide cites about $35 by Uber (23 min) and the CapMetro 20 bus at $1.25; the source doesn’t say which trip those fares cover.
  • Downtown is about 15 miles from COTA.
  • Uber/Lyft fares spike after the race.

Lodging

One guide (checked Sept 28) estimates downtown hotels at about $260 a night normally versus roughly $700–$1,500 during the Grand Prix, before 15% tax. Airport-area hotels are closer and cheaper; Round Rock and Pflugerville are cheaper still but need a car and are up to 19 miles out. These are estimates, not quotes.

Forecast

I couldn’t find a reliable day-by-day forecast for Oct 22–25 when this was compiled (Oct 17). Typical mid-October Austin: highs about 70–80°F, lows down to about 60°F (austin.gp). AccuWeather’s monthly range for Oct 2026 runs 71–90°F highs and 45–66°F lows. It’s rain or shine at COTA.

The fine print

Know Before You Go

Payment

COTA is cashless. The race-weekend guide lists Visa, Discover and Mastercard; the venue policies page also lists American Express.

First aid

Grand Plaza, Main Grandstand, and the Paddock medical center.

Alcohol

21+ with valid ID (checked if you look 30 or younger). 2-drink limit per ID per purchase. Sales stop about 30 minutes before the event ends.

Need help?

Text COTA + your question to 69050 (include your location). Emergencies: call 911 or flag any COTA staff.

Tickets

Mobile tickets through the COTA app (parking passes and 3-day GA wristbands are the exceptions). Register your GA wristband before race weekend on the Frontgate Tickets page; once cinched it won’t loosen. All sales final. Resale on property is prohibited.

Lost & found

Guest Services tents (between the Main Entry Gates and Grand Plaza); QR codes there to file a report. Items held 14 days.

Kids

Children 3 and older need a ticket for F1.

Accessibility

Accessible parking in Lots A, D, H. Accessible shuttles: text COTA to 69050. ADA golf carts at ADA tents. Sensory rooms in Grand Plaza. Accessible grandstand seating at Main Grandstand, Turn 12, Turn 15.

Box offices

Grand Plaza (opens Mon Oct 19): Mon–Thu 10–5, Fri & Sat 8:30–8:00, Sun 8:30–5.
Main Grandstand: Fri 9:30–4:50, Sat 8:30–4:30, Sun 8:30–2:30.

Nursing & restrooms

Nursing pods: Main Grandstand, Grand Plaza, Turn 1, Turn 3. Permanent restrooms: Grand Plaza, Amphitheater bowl, Main Grandstand, Turn 1.

App

Download the COTA app for maps, schedules and food locations. COTA says to update it in settings on Wednesday, Oct 21.

Car trouble

Free jump-starts until one hour after the event: 512-655-6220. No lockout help.

Food

BBQ, Tex-Mex, pizza, Turkish wraps, Thai, vegan and vegetarian, plus local food trucks. One older review lists a beer at $19.

Severe weather

Rain or shine. Lightning triggers shelter announcements on the PA and video boards. Schedule changes post to social media and the app.
